Bean Chilli Mix

Description:

This is a recipe for a Bean Chilli Mix, which tastes best when used with a wrap. Optionally, but again, recommended, is the addition of sour cream, cheese, and lettuce. For a vegan alternative, replace sour cream and cheese with coconut yogurt and your favourite vegan cheese. (Maybe you could make some Guacamole to really fill up a wrap!

Ingredients:

  • 2 tins black beans
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlice, finely chopped
  • 1 tspn paprika
  • 1 tspn cumin
  • 1 tspn chilli flakes
  • 1 tspn turmeric
  • 3 tbspn olive oil
  • 50 grams coriander
  • lime juice and zest of one lime (zest optional)
  • 300 grams cherry tomatoes

Steps:

Tip: Before you begin, be sure to put all spices into a single pot!

  1. Pour 2 tablespoons of olive oil into a pan on medium heat, then toss in the onions. Stir at a medium heat for 5 minutes.
  2. Add garlic, continue stirring for 1 minute, add spices, and stir for a final 30 seconds.
  3. Pour in the tinned chopped tomatoes and beans. If intended to be eaten WITH a wrap, strain the beans with the tin lid or a sieve first. At this point, reduce the heat to a simmer, occasionally stirring, for 15 minutes
  4. Allow to cool a little, then add half the coriander and half the lime juice.
  5. Chop the cherry tomatoes into quarters or eighths to taste, and pour into a separate bowl
  6. Pour the remaining olive oil, lime juice, and coriander into the bowl
  7. Yayy!!!!!
