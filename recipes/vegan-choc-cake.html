Vegan Chocolate Cake

Description:

Now that you've had your dinner, it's time for something fun! This is a recipe for a delicious vegan chocolate cake to totally stuff you if our other recipes didn't manage that already.

Ingredients:

  • 360ml flour
  • 240ml white sugar
  • 60ml cocoa powder
  • 1 tspn baking soda
  • ½ tspn salt
  • 240ml water
  • 80ml vegetable oil
  • 1 tspn vanilla extract
  • 1 tspn distilled white vinegar

Steps:

Tip: Serve with berries for a refined touch.

  1. Preheat oven to 175 degrees C (350 F). Lightly grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Sift flour, sugar, cocoa, baking soda, and salt together in a large bowl.
  3. Add water, oil, vanilla, and vinegar. Mix together until smooth.
  4. Pour mixture into the prepared pan
  5. Bake in the oven for approx. 45 minutes. Test by inserting a toothpick or small knife into the center. If it comes out clean, the cake is done!
  6. Serve.
