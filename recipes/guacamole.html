Guacamole

Description:

This is a short recipe for spiced Guacamole, which will go excellently as an addition to the Bean Chilli Wrap recipe also available on this website!

Ingredients:

  • Two peeled, pitted avocados
  • 1 Chopped small onion
  • 1 Chopped tomato
  • One clove minced or diced garlic
  • Salt & pepper
  • Lime juice

Steps:

  1. Mash avocado into a large bowl.
  2. Stir in onion, tomato, and garlic.
  3. Season with lime juice, salt, and pepper.
  4. If prepared sufficiently in advance, cover and chill for 30 minutes to allow flavors to blend.
